# Neumorphism surface recipes for WPF

The [Awesome Neumorphism collection](https://github.com/jqueryscript/awesome-neumorphism) covers CSS and React component libraries, React Native and Swift rendering, design-kit previews, a shadow generator, and icon sets. The recipes below adapt those patterns to WPF and Material Design 3.

## Model the light and the surface

| Treatment | WPF implementation | Where it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat | Use a neutral fill and add an outline or state marker as needed. | Navigation, list and grid rows, labels, dense toolbars. |
| Raised | Match the face to its surrounding neutral role. Put two rounded silhouettes behind the real face, each with one `DropShadowEffect`. Offset the highlight toward the upper left and the shade toward the lower right. Keep the content above both layers. | A prominent button, slider thumb, or standalone panel. |
| Recessed | Layer two shallow edge gradients inside matching rounded geometry. Shade the upper-left inner edge and highlight the lower-right inner edge. Clip only the edge layers to the rounded shape. | Text fields, slider tracks, switch wells, and pressed controls. |
| Convex or concave face | Add a shallow gradient to the face. A brighter upper-left face suggests convex curvature. Reversing the gradient suggests concave curvature. Keep the lighting axis aligned with the edge treatment. | A large knob or other control with room to show curvature. |

Treat offset, blur, opacity, and corner radius as separate parameters. Tune depth in DIPs at the control's actual size. Give outer shadows space and keep focus adorners clear of clipped edges. A rounded WPF border provides the silhouette for an outer effect. For rounded clipping on an inner effect, use a matching geometry. Try gradient edges or flat surfaces for frequently repeated items where paired bitmap effects make scrolling expensive. [Neumorphism generator](https://github.com/adamgiebl/neumorphism) · [Neumorphic Elements](https://codepen.io/myacode/pen/PoqQQNM) · [Neomorph UI Kit](https://codepen.io/miocene/pen/oNNOmza) · [WPF DropShadowEffect](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.windows.media.effects.dropshadoweffect)

## Keep states legible

- **Buttons and toggles.** Let a normal action sit flush or rise slightly. Move its edge treatment inward while pressed without changing its size or hit area. A selected toggle needs a persistent signal such as a moved thumb, selected glyph, or marked segment. For icon-only actions, preserve the recognizable silhouette and give the native control an automation name. [EMTNeumorphicView](https://github.com/hirokimu/EMTNeumorphicView) · [SYMBIO media and toggle controls](https://dribbble.com/shots/9956580-Freebie-Neumorphic-UI-elements) · [Agente icon set](https://agentestudio.com/blog/neumorphism-icon-set)
- **Fields, sliders, and progress.** Separate the control from its surrounding surface with a readable outline or edge. Use a distinct value fill and an identifiable thumb or endpoint. Keep the caret, placeholder labels, validation messages, and focus indicator readable on the actual field fill. A status value or label helps when a progress fill is subtle. [Neumorphic Elements](https://codepen.io/myacode/pen/PoqQQNM) · [Themesberg control library](https://github.com/themesberg/neumorphism-ui-bootstrap) · [Kevin Al-Rizal's dark controls](https://dribbble.com/shots/11652074-FREE-Neumorphism-UI-Kit)
- **Light and dark themes.** Preserve offset directions and derive tonal offsets anew from the active seed and surface. Aim for visibly different tones on both sides of the face. In dark mode, tune the lighter edge against the neutral face before increasing shadow opacity. Use paired Material Design 3 foreground and fill roles for text and accents, and check the resulting colors after opacities and gradients are composited. [UI-Neumorphism theme variables](https://github.com/AKAspanion/ui-neumorphism) · [React Native Neomorph Shadows](https://github.com/tokkozhin/react-native-neomorph-shadows) · [Neomorphic Darkness](https://www.behance.net/gallery/90329723/Neomorphic-Darkness-Free-UI-Kit)

Review states with relief removed to check that role, value, selection, and focus still read. Test light and dark modes with a muted seed and a saturated seed, Windows high contrast, and increased display scaling. Check contrast for the visible text, the mark identifying a state, and the edge that identifies a control. Decorative shadows can stay soft. [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) · [WCAG non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) · [WCAG focus visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html)
